Put one PNG per card here, cropped from a frame of your own hand at your phone's resolution, named after the card:
`knight.png`, `archers.png`, `fireball.png`, … The `hand` read matches each of the four slots against these.
Get frames with `anygame record --device adb://<phone-ip>:5555 --out packs/clash-royale/fixtures --pack clash-royale`
and crop with any image editor. Four to eight crops is a deck; that is the whole "training".
